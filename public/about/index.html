ABOUT THE STORE

A local grocery shop, with pickup ordering online

Mana Kirana is the online catalogue for Venkata Kusuma Haranth General Stores. Customers can check the shopkeeper’s listed items and prices, request an item that is missing from the catalogue, and place an order to collect at the shop.

01

Browse the shop’s catalogue

The shopkeeper maintains the shared list of products, selling units, prices and stock. The customer catalogue reflects those entries.

02

Ask for an unlisted item

Customers can add a request for something not shown. The shopkeeper confirms its price before it is included in the bill.

03

Collect at the store

Orders are for in-store pickup. The app shows an expected ready time, and the shopkeeper checks the customer’s four-digit pickup PIN at collection.

04

Pay when you pick up

This site supports pickup ordering; it does not take online payments. Pay at the store when collecting your order.
